Starter $6/mo 30k credits includes commercial licence. Instant cloning from Starter; Professional cloning Creator $22/mo with voice captcha verification. Students get Creator free 3 months; no instructor discount verified. Trains on submitted data by default; opt-out in account settings (Terms and Privacy > Data use), forward-only.
3. **Timing data.** TTS: `POST /v1/text-to-speech/{voice_id}/with-timestamps` returns `audio_base64` + `alignment`/`normalized_alignment` (per-char start/end seconds). Dialogue: `POST /v1/text-to-dialogue/with-timestamps` exists, returns alignment + `voice_segments[]` (voice_id, start/end seconds, char indices, dialogue_input_index). Dialogue limit: keep total text <= 2,000 chars. Continuity: TTS uses `previous_text`/`next_text`; dialogue uses `previous_text`/`future_text` (<=100 chars each), "not supported by every model". `output_format` is a query param (default mp3_44100_128).
